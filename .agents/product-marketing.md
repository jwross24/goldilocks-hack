# Goldilocks — Product Marketing Context

> Source-of-truth doc. All marketing skills (copywriting, launch, customer-research, etc.) should read this before generating anything.

## What the product is

Goldilocks is an AI shopping agent that reads furniture dimensions, materials, and reviews against a customer's specific body, life stage, and constraints — then tells the truth, by the inch.

It is not an accessibility tool, a personal stylist, a chatbot, or a Wayfair clone. It is a precision shopping layer for customers whose bodies don't fit median product assumptions.

## Who it's for

The unifying customer truth: *"I want someone to do the math for me."*

Five archetype personas (extensible):

1. **Maya, 47** — manual wheelchair user. Needs seat height in the 17-19″ transfer range. JTBD: *Find a sofa I can independently get out of.* Currently buys + returns or buys + suffers.
2. **Carlos, 52** — L4-L5 lumbar fusion. Needs 19-22″ to stand without strain. JTBD: *Find a sofa that doesn't flare my back in 10 minutes.* Currently sits in a recliner he hates.
3. **Eleanor, 75** — arthritis (hands + knees), uses a cane. Needs firm seat 18-20″ + armrest leverage. JTBD: *Keep my independence — don't make me ask for help to stand up.* Currently delays buying because she can't trust spec sheets.
4. **Sam, 6'5"** — tall engineer. Needs deeper-than-standard seats + higher backs + verified weight capacity. JTBD: *Stop buying sofas that feel like child furniture.* Currently buys oversized and returns.
5. **Priya, 32** — six weeks post-C-section, new parent. Needs firm support + reachable depth + spill-resilient material. JTBD: *A sofa I can recover on AND feed a baby on AND not panic when she spits up.* Currently sitting in pain on the old sectional.

These are not "accessibility personas." They are **bodies the median sofa was not designed for**. The category is *fit at scale*.

## The pain (verbatim register)

Real customer language — paraphrased from Reddit, Trustpilot, and BBB Wayfair complaints + r/wheelchair + r/backpain communities:

- "I bought three sofas before I found one I could stand up from."
- "The dimensions on the website are useless. Seat height isn't even listed."
- "It looked great in the photo. The seat is 14 inches. I'm 6'5"."
- "After surgery I needed something firm. Everything is marshmallow."
- "It says 'ADA-compliant.' Compliant with what? It's a sofa."

The pattern: **the dimensions that decide a purchase are buried, missing, or marketed away.**

## The category position

**Wayfair sells sofas. Goldilocks sells fits.**

That single sentence is the brand. Wayfair has 22M customers and ~$12B revenue — millions of those customers are making purchases against spec sheets that hide the dimension they care about. Goldilocks turns dimensions into the headline.

## What it explicitly is not

Anti-positioning (avoid all of these):

- An accessibility-only tool → it's *fit for everybody*
- A chatbot → it's a measurement layer
- A personal stylist → it doesn't care about taste, it cares about specs
- A buyer's guide → it makes the decision, doesn't list options
- A Wayfair clone → it's a layer that could live on Wayfair, Amazon, Article, anywhere
- A disability brand → don't pity, don't symbolize, don't soft-pastel
- A premium luxury brand → it's $800 sofas, not $5,000 ones
- A startup landing page → no gradients, no rotating logos, no "powered by AI"

## How customers describe the value (target verbatim)

- "Goldilocks just told me the number I'd been looking for."
- "It said TOO LOW. I trusted it because it showed me the inches off."
- "The sofa it picked fit me on the first try."

Voice goal: customers feeling **respected by the specificity**.

## The brand personality (3 words)

**Calm · exacting · warm.**

The exactness is the respect. Validated by 2-of-3 unbiased subagent passes; 1 dissent picked "unhurried" which we incorporated as pacing in the UI (the silence move on match).

## The aesthetic North Star

**Apartamento meets Linear.** Editorial typography + Linear-grade monospaced numerics. Light warm off-white paper. No dark AI dashboard. Read `.impeccable.md` for full visual spec.

## Pricing posture (future)

Goldilocks should remain free to the end customer. Revenue from retailer integrations or affiliate model. The customer trust depends on *we work for you, not the retailer* — never feature paid placements.

## The single brand-defining move

**Treat measurements like a fashion house treats its logotype.** Numbers are not buried in 11pt gray spec tables. They are the headline. `18.5″` rendered with the care Hermès gives its H.
